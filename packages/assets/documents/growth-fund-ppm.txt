InvestRI Growth Fund I — Private Placement Memorandum (ILLUSTRATIVE / FICTIONAL)

This PPM is a demonstration placeholder. It is not an offering of securities.
No real subscription can be accepted through this proof of concept.

1. The offering
InvestRI Growth Fund I is an illustrative, professionally managed private-markets
vehicle designed to put Rhode Island resident capital to work in housing, small
business lending, historic reuse, manufacturing, innovation, and resiliency.

2. Who may subscribe
This demonstration is limited to Rhode Island residents who complete identity,
residency, and suitability steps. Minimum subscription is $100. Default
demonstration amount is $1,000.

3. Use of proceeds
Subscription proceeds in a live offering would be deployed across the fund’s
stated allocation. In this POC, no money moves and no securities are issued.

4. Risk
You can lose some or all of your principal. Interests are illiquid. Target
returns of 7%–10% are illustrations, not promises.

5. Tax credit (proposed, not investment return)
Any Rhode Island tax incentive shown in the application is a proposed program
configuration. It is not enacted law, not cash back, and not part of investment
performance. Do not subscribe because of a tax credit.

6. Documents you should read
Read this PPM together with the subscription agreement, investor fact sheet,
risk factors, and tax disclosure before you sign.
